# Reception 2.0 — Manager Onboarding Scope

## Purpose

Reception 2.0 is the single working home for training and supporting the Urban Muaythai Front Desk Manager.

It should help the manager:

- learn the role in a clear order;
- run each shift without relying on memory;
- make routine decisions confidently;
- find the correct procedure quickly;
- know when and how to escalate;
- train and check front desk staff;
- leave a complete handover; and
- keep operational records in the approved system.

## Operating model

Reception 2.0 should be the Front of House / Gym Manager's command page. It should explain:

- what the manager is responsible for;
- what must be checked daily, weekly and when triggered;
- which tool or existing guide to open;
- what a completed result looks like; and
- when the manager must escalate to Ram, Mel or another approved owner.

Detailed procedures do not need to be copied into the command page when an approved page already exists. Reception 2.0 should link to that page, explain when to use it and state the manager's responsibility for the outcome.

Example:

> **Clubworx Analysis** — Reception 2.0 tells the manager when it must be completed, who completes it, what must be reviewed and how completion is confirmed. The dedicated Clubworx Analysis page contains the detailed audit steps.

## Role and responsibility split

### Front desk team

- Complete the assigned shift checklist and member-facing tasks.
- Use the approved procedure for each action.
- Record work in Clubworx or the approved tracker.
- Leave a clear handover for anything unfinished.
- Escalate unclear policy, account, billing, complaint or safety matters.

### Front of House / Gym Manager

- Own the standard and completion of front desk work.
- Allocate the shift, daily and weekly duties.
- Confirm staff have access to and can use the required tools.
- Train, observe and sign off staff against real tasks.
- Review handovers, overdue tasks and incomplete member actions.
- Check that Clubworx, inboxes and approved trackers tell the same story.
- Correct authorised errors and escalate matters outside the manager's authority.
- Maintain the SOP links and flag missing, conflicting or outdated instructions.
- Report recurring errors, risks, member issues and operational blockers.

### Ram / Mel / approved senior owner

- Decide policy, pricing and authority limits.
- Resolve escalated billing, refund, membership and serious complaint matters.
- Approve disputed or changed procedures.
- Confirm performance expectations and reporting requirements.

## Manager knowledge areas to build

1. Role ownership and standards
   - What the manager owns, what staff own and what requires Ram or Mel.
   - Service, presentation, response-time and record-keeping standards.

2. Shift control
   - Opening, peak-time, class check-in, closing and handover.
   - How to prioritise when several members or tasks need attention.

3. People leadership
   - Staff onboarding, demonstrations, observation and sign-off.
   - Shift allocation, coaching, corrections, accountability and backup cover.

4. Member service and retention
   - Enquiries, trials, new members, attendance concerns and common complaints.
   - Suspensions, cancellations, upgrades, downgrades and sensitive conversations.

5. Clubworx and record quality
   - Required notes, tasks, follow-up dates and ownership.
   - How to avoid duplicate, incomplete or unsupported member actions.

6. Payments and sales
   - Failed and pending payments, EFTPOS, equipment sales, memberships and offers.
   - Clear limits on what reception may promise, change or refund.

7. Escalations and incidents
   - Account uncertainty, payment disputes, injuries, safeguarding, complaints and emergencies.
   - Who to contact, what to record and what must not wait.

8. Weekly manager rhythm
   - Staff checks, unresolved handovers, overdue follow-ups, stock, cleaning and facility issues.
   - Reporting, review and preparation for the next week.

9. Reference library
   - Current approved procedures, prices, forms, scripts, videos and contacts.
   - Owners and review dates so outdated material is easy to identify.

## Design rules

- Put the most common actions first.
- Organise work by the manager's real shift and weekly rhythm.
- Use short checklists, decision paths and examples.
- Every task must state: owner, trigger, steps, done-when rule, record location and escalation point.
- Separate training guidance from live member records.
- Do not store member cases, credentials or payment details in this repository.
- Do not invent policy when source instructions conflict; label it for confirmation.
- Keep the page usable on a reception computer and easy to scan during a busy shift.

## Usage-saving working rules

- Treat this folder as the Reception 2.0 project unless Ram changes the scope.
- Query `graphify-out` before rereading large project files.
- Inspect only the files and source sections needed for the current task.
- Reuse verified procedures and existing assets before creating replacements.
- Batch related checks and avoid repeating completed audits.
- State what is verified, proposed, missing or awaiting confirmation.
- Work in small reviewable sections, starting with the manager's highest-frequency duties.
